What this means for the store

  • Stock bangles ahead of July and October; these two months are the largest single risk and opportunity.
  • Plan wool purchases for November to February and cut orders after winter.
  • Order Class C items only on demand; together they are about 8% of revenue.
  • Start recording wholesale customers and push digital payments (cash is 95% of transactions).
Source: store sales registers, one year only, so seasonal patterns describe this year and are not a validated forecast. Units differ across products, so compare revenue, not quantity. Gross profit = revenue minus cost of goods sold.
